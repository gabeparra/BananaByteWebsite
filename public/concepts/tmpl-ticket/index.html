The Headliner

Hi, I'm Dani.

I'm a stand-up out of Orlando who never met a quiet front row I couldn't ruin in the nicest way possible. I built my hour the hard way — open mics on Tuesdays, road gigs on Sundays, and roughly a thousand strangers who said "I'm just here for someone's birthday" and left as a recurring character.

My thing is crowd work: real conversations that turn into the best part of the night. No two shows are the same, which is terrifying for me and great for you. I've shared stages from Florida theaters to New York basements, and I'm bringing the new hour everywhere a club will let me plug in a mic.

Off stage I'm a recovering middle child, a mediocre runner, and the only person at the taco truck who tips like the economy is fine.
