About

Hi, I'm Dani

I started doing open mics in a Mills 50 dive bar that smelled like spilled beer and ambition, and somehow that turned into a career. Six years later I'm headlining clubs across the Southeast — still chasing the same thing: the room where everyone forgets their week for an hour.

My specialty is crowd work. I'd rather find out you're an orthodontist from Sanford who hates cilantro than run the exact same set twice. Every show is a little different, which is terrifying and also the whole point.

When I'm not on the road, I produce a monthly showcase in Orlando and teach a beginner stand-up workshop for people brave enough to bomb in public. Come say hi after a set — I'm nicer than I look on stage.
