About Rico

They started calling him The Avalanche in a smoke-filled VFW hall in Spokane — after he buried three men under a single corner splash and the whole front row felt the floor shake. Twelve years later, the name fits better than ever.

Rico Marsh is a 285-pound force of nature who moves like a man half his size and hits like a freight train. A throwback powerhouse with a high-flyer's appetite for risk, he's headlined coast to coast, drawn rave reviews, and walked out of every steel cage he's ever entered with the belt over his shoulder.
