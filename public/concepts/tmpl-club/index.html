About Dani

Dani Cole is an Orlando stand-up who treats the front row like a scene partner — and the back row like a jury. After ten years of open mics, bar shows, and the occasional wedding she swears she was invited to, she's built a reputation for turning a quiet Tuesday crowd into the loudest room in the building.

Her comedy lives in the small stuff: the group chat that won't die, the self-checkout that calls for help, the friend who's "doing great" in a tone that says otherwise. No bit is too local; no audience is too tired. If you've got a story, she's already three jokes ahead of it.

She headlines clubs across the Southeast, hosts the monthly "Cole Open" showcase in Orlando, and is currently developing an hour about turning thirty-something and meaning it.
