About Dani

Warm room, sharp tongue.

Dani Cole has spent the last eight years figuring out exactly how much to mess with an audience before they love you for it. The answer, it turns out, is "a little more than that." Based in Orlando, she's built a reputation as one of the most fearless crowd-work comics on the circuit — equal parts therapist, talk-show host, and friend who tells you the truth at brunch.

Her debut hour, "Two-Drink Minimum," sold out three weekends in Florida before she ever taped it. She's headlined from the Comedy Cellar to the Punch Line, opened for names she's still not allowed to print, and somehow keeps getting invited back to her own hometown. She makes the back of the room feel like the front row — and the front row regret nothing.
