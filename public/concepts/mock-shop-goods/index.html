Our story

Made slow, made by hand

"We started in a garage with a single thermometer and a thrift-store percolator. The recipe hasn't changed much — and neither has the patience."

Ember & Oak began when Nora and Theo Vance couldn't find a candle that smelled like their grandmother's kitchen — or a coffee worth pairing it with. So they made both. Every candle is still poured in small batches, cooled overnight, and trimmed by hand before it ever reaches a shelf.

Clean-burning wax

Coconut-soy blend with cotton wicks — no paraffin, no synthetic dye.

Roasted in small lots

Coffee roasted weekly in 9-lb batches and shipped within 48 hours.
