🙋 The Short Version

Hi, I'm Dani. I talk to strangers for a living.

Some comics write five years of jokes. I write five years of jokes and then ignore them the second a guy named Brad makes eye contact.

Born and raised in Orlando, I cut my teeth at open mics between theme-park shifts, learned that the best material is sitting in row two pretending not to make eye contact, and never looked back. My sets are part stand-up, part interview, part group therapy nobody signed up for.

These days I headline clubs and festivals across the Southeast with a brand-new hour, "Nacho Problem." It's a different show every night, because you're a different crowd every night. Come early, sit close, and absolutely do not lie about your job.
