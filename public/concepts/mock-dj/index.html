The Signal Behind NOVA

NOVA is the electronic alias of a producer who treats the dancefloor like a circuit board — every kick a voltage spike, every breakdown a held breath before the surge.

Born out of late-night warehouse sessions and a love for modular synths, NOVA's sound fuses hypnotic melodic techno with raw acid energy. Six years deep, a residency at Pulse, and a discography that keeps the bass bins humming till sunrise.
