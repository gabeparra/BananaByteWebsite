About

The warm one with teeth

Dani Cole is an Orlando-based stand-up who turns a quiet Tuesday crowd into the best night out a stranger has had in months.

Raised between Florida and "wherever the work was," Dani built a reputation the slow way: a thousand open mics, a few brutal road weeks, and a knack for reading a room faster than the room can read itself. She is best known for crowd work that feels less like a roast and more like getting adopted by the funniest person at the bar.

Her sets are warm but sharp, clean enough for a corporate gala and loose enough for a 10pm basement show. She has opened for national headliners, sold out hometown rooms, and made at least one wedding toast that people still bring up.
