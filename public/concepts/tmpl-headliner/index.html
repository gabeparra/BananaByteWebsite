03 — About

Meet Dani

Dani Cole

Orlando, Florida · Est. 2016

  • 9Yrs on stage
  • 340+Shows / yr
  • 0Hecklers won

Dani Cole grew up in Central Florida, the kind of place where the weather, the tourists, and your relatives all give you plenty of material. She started doing open mics in 2016 and never quite stopped — these days she headlines clubs across the Southeast and treats every front row like an unpaid co-writer.

Her thing is crowd work. No two Dani Cole shows are the same, because half of each one is built live from whoever decided to sit close to the stage. It's warm, it's quick, and it never punches down — she's the friend who roasts you because she actually likes you.

When she's not on the road, you'll find her hosting Orlando showcases, taping new hours, and explaining to her mom (again) that yes, this is a real job.
