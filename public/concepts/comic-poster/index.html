About Dani

Dani Cole is a stand-up comedian based in Orlando, Florida — the kind of comic who would rather talk to you than at you. What started as open mics above a sandwich shop turned into a touring crowd-work act built on one simple idea: everybody in the room is funnier than they think, and her job is to prove it.

Her sets are warm without being soft and sharp without being mean. She'll find the couple on their third date, the guy who clearly got dragged there, the table that thinks they're invisible — and she'll make all of them the heroes of the night. No two shows are the same, which is exactly the point.

When she's not on the road, Dani hosts a monthly showcase in Orlando and writes way too much in the notes app at 2 a.m. She has been featured on regional comedy festivals and headlines clubs across the Southeast.
