The story

A little warm, a lot fast

Dani Cole is an Orlando stand-up who built a name the hard way — open mics, late sets, and a refusal to ever do the same show twice. The crowd is the co-writer. Give Dani a front row and a heckler and the night writes itself.

Five years in, Dani has headlined clubs across the Southeast, opened festival rooms, and turned a tourist town's worst small talk into some of its sharpest material. The act is warm without being soft, quick without being mean — the kind of room you leave grinning.

When not on stage, Dani is probably eavesdropping on the next table. It's research.
