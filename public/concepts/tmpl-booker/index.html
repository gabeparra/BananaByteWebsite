About

Meet Dani

Dani Cole is an Orlando-based stand-up who built a name on the most dangerous part of any show: talking to actual people.

A decade of late sets, open mics, and packed Friday rooms turned a nervous improviser into one of the Southeast's most reliable crowd-work headliners. The jokes are written, but the best ones happen live — born from a name tag, a bad first date in row two, or whoever decided to check their phone during the punchline.

Warm, quick, and never mean for the sake of it, Dani plays clubs, theaters, colleges, and corporate nights that want a comic who reads the room instead of bulldozing it.
