About

An image is a decision, not an accident.

I'm Maya — a photographer and art director working out of a sun-filled studio in the Mills 50 district of Orlando.

For the last decade I've helped brands and publications say something true with one frame. My background in art direction means I think past the shutter: mood boards, casting, set, the edit. The work tends to be warm, unhurried, and a little bit honest.

When I'm not shooting, you'll find me sourcing thrifted props, chasing the 6 p.m. light along Lake Eola, or testing film stocks I probably can't justify.
