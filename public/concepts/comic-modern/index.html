About Dani

Warm, sharp, and genuinely curious about your night

Dani Cole is a stand-up comedian based in Orlando, Florida, known for the kind of crowd work that makes strangers feel like the funniest version of themselves.

After a decade of late shows, open mics, and one very memorable wedding gig, Dani built a reputation for turning the front row into the whole show — quick, kind, never cheap. The jokes land because they're observed, not aimed. People leave feeling like they were in on it.

Her hour blends tight written material with the live, unrepeatable stuff that only happens once: the dentist who heckled, the couple on a third date, the guy who admitted he sells trampolines. Bookers know what they're getting — a clean, reliable headliner who reads a room and runs on time.
