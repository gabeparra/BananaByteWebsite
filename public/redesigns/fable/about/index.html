The short version

So here’s the honest pitch: I’m one person who cares a lot about the web, and I’d rather build a handful of sites I’m genuinely proud of than crank out a hundred I’m not. BANANAbyte is small on purpose — that’s the feature, not the caveat.

01Why I started BANANAbyte

I started BANANAbyte because I kept seeing good local businesses stuck with websites that didn’t sound like them — a template someone bolted their name onto and left. I wanted to do the opposite: sit with a person, understand what they actually do, and build the whole thing, design and code, from the ground up.

02Why Orlando

Orlando’s more than the parks, and the people who make it interesting — the taco spot two blocks over, the corner boutique, the comedian working the local room — are exactly who I want to build for. This is home, so when I say “local,” I mean I’ll actually come sit at your counter.

03One opinion I’ll die on

My one strong opinion: a website that makes you fight a pop-up before you can read it has already failed. Fast, quiet, and it works on the cheapest phone in the room — that’s the whole job. Everything clever comes after that, or not at all.
