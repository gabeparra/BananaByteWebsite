The short version

So here's the honest pitch: I'm one person who cares a lot about the web, and I'd rather build a handful of sites I'm genuinely proud of than crank out a hundred I'm not. BANANAbyte is small on purpose — that's the feature, not the caveat.

01Why I started BANANAbyte

I started BANANAbyte because I kept seeing good local businesses stuck with websites that didn't sound like them — a template someone bolted their name onto and left. I wanted to do the opposite: sit with a person, understand what they actually do, and build the whole thing, design and code, from the ground up.

02Why Orlando

Orlando's more than the parks, and the people who make it interesting — the taco spot two blocks over, the corner boutique, the comedian working the local room — are exactly who I want to build for. This is home, so when I say "local," I mean I'll actually come sit at your counter.

03The Peter Hefty story

My first real client was Peter Hefty, an award-winning ventriloquist and comedian who needed a booking site with as much personality as his act. We built him a red-curtain, theatrical home on the web — and when he told me it felt like watching a magician pull his new website out of thin air, that's the moment I knew this was the work I wanted to keep doing.

04One opinion I'll die on

My one strong opinion: a website that makes you fight a pop-up before you can read it has already failed. Fast, quiet, and it works on the cheapest phone in the room — that's the whole job. Everything clever comes after that, or not at all.

Being honest about it

A new studio, run by one person. Here's exactly what that means.

I'd rather you know precisely what you're getting than oversell it. Small and new comes with real trade-offs — and real advantages. Both are on the table.

What you get

Depth, not scale.

  • The same person designs, builds and answers your email — start to finish.
  • Your project gets real attention, not a slot in an agency's conveyor belt.
  • Straight answers, one flat price quoted upfront, and you own everything at the end.
  • Local and bilingual — we can do the whole thing in English or Spanish.
What I won't pretend

No smoke, no mirrors.

  • No fake team page — it's me. No stock-photo "account executives."
  • No invented "20 years" or a shelf of awards — BANANAbyte is genuinely new.
  • No wall of logos I didn't build. One real client, shown proudly — and honestly.
