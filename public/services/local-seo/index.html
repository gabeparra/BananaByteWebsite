What you get

Everything it takes to land on the map — and stay there.

Hands-on, done-for-you work on your Google presence — nothing automated or generic. I set up, optimize, and clean up every detail so local people find you first.

Google Business Profile, done right

I claim or create your profile and fill it out properly: the right categories, description, hours, service areas, and links. This listing decides whether you show on the map, so I build it carefully.

Photos, hours & service areas

I add and arrange good photos, keep your hours and holiday days current, and define the areas you cover — so anyone who finds you instantly knows what you offer and where.

On-page local SEO

I tune your site for local search: titles, descriptions, consistent contact details, and clear service and location pages — so Google connects your business to your city.

Local keyword targeting

I research how people in your area actually search — “your service near me,” by neighborhood or by city — and point your profile and site at those real terms instead of guessing.

Review-generation help

I set you up with a simple way to ask for reviews — a direct link, a QR code, and a short script — so good reviews come in steadily. Reviews carry real weight on the map.

Bilingual listings & content

Where it makes sense, I set up your listing and content in both English and Spanish — so in Orlando you’re found and understood by people searching in either language.

Who it’s for

If nearby people should find you and don’t, this is for you.

This is built for local businesses that live on nearby customers but don’t show up — or show up buried — when someone searches Google. If you depend on being found in your area, ranking on the map is worth real money.

How it works

From invisible to on the map, in four steps.

We start by seeing where you stand today, get your presence in great shape, and — if you want — I keep it cared for month to month. You work directly with me and always know what was done and why.

1

Audit

I check how you show up on Google today — your profile, your site, your reviews, and who’s outranking you locally — and tell you plainly what’s holding your visibility back.

2

Set up the profile

I claim or create your Google Business Profile and get it complete and verified: categories, description, hours, service areas, photos, and contact details all in place.

3

Optimize

I tune your site and listing for the local searches that matter, keep everything consistent between Google and your site, and set up a simple system to gather reviews.

4

Ongoing

If you want, I stay on to keep it cared for: posts, fresh photos, review replies, and a simple monthly report on how you’re climbing. It’s optional — the setup is already yours.
