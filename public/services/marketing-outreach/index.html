What you get

Everything it takes to get you in front of the right people — without you doing the legwork.

Hands-on, done-for-you work: I research, I reach out, I set up the ads, and I get your listings in order. You tell me what you do and who you want to reach; I handle the moving parts.

A real marketing plan

I research where your customers or bookers actually are — which channels, which events, which directories — and build a concrete plan instead of guessing. We start by knowing who you want to reach and where they already hang out.

Outreach, done for you

I find AND contact the right venues, bookers, press, partners, and directories on your behalf. I write the pitch, follow up, and pass you whatever comes back — you never have to send the awkward first email.

Ads set up & run

I create your ad accounts on Google, Meta, and local media, target them at the right people, and manage them on honest, small budgets. No burning money — we start small and scale what works.

Listings & profiles

I set up and keep consistent your Google Business Profile, the event or industry directories, and your social bios — same name, same details, same link everywhere, so people find you with no confusion.

The research + contact list, yours to keep

I hand you what I found: who’s worth knowing, the pitch that works, and the assets ready to use. Even if we stop working together one day, the contact list stays with you.

Bilingual outreach

I reach both of Orlando’s markets: the English-speaking one AND the Spanish-speaking one. Outreach, ads, and listings in both languages — twice the market without paying double or hiring two people.

If you’re a performer who wants to get booked, or a local business that wants more people through the door, this is for you. I do the outreach, the ads, and the listings so the work comes to you instead of you going out to chase it.

How it works

From nobody knowing you to people coming in, in four steps.

We start with your goals, I build the plan, I set it up and do the first outreach round, and — if you want — I keep it moving month to month. You work directly with me and always know what was done and why.

1

We map it

We start with your goals, your audience, and where they already are. You tell me what you do and who you want to reach, and I map out where it makes sense to start.

2

I build the plan

I research the right channels and the right people — venues, bookers, directories, and ads. You get a concrete plan of who to contact, where to advertise, and what to say.

3

I set it up + reach out

I get the ads and listings live, and I do the first outreach round myself: writing, sending, and following up with the venues, partners, and press on your list.

4

Ongoing

If you want, I keep reaching out, manage the ads, and send you a simple monthly report on what’s working. It’s optional — the setup and the contacts are already yours.
