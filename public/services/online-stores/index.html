What you get

A store that’s ready to sell — not a half-built kit.

Every store ships complete: the design, the catalog, the payments, and everything needed for that first order to land clean on launch day.

Custom storefront design

A design built around your brand and your products — not a generic template you’ve seen on ten other shops. A homepage, collections, and product pages that make people want to buy.

Product catalog

Your products loaded for real — photos, descriptions, prices, variants (size, color), collections and filters. I leave the catalog set up and easy for you to expand yourself.

Secure checkout & payments Secure

Encrypted, PCI-compliant checkout through Shopify Payments — cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay. You never touch card data, and customers pay in a couple of taps.

Shipping & tax set up

Shipping rates, zones and label printing wired up, plus automatic sales-tax collection based on where you sell. No guessing shipping costs or owing tax surprises at year-end.

Inventory basics

Stock counts, low-stock alerts, and the option to auto-hide sold-out items. I show you how to update it in minutes so you never sell something you no longer have.

Mobile-first, for real

Most of your buyers arrive on a phone, so the store is designed there first: it loads fast, the buttons land where a thumb can reach, and checkout never breaks on a small screen.

You own the store

The Shopify account, the domain, the products, the customers — all in your name from day one. You can run it solo, bring in someone else, or walk away with everything in hand whenever you like.

How it works

From idea to first sale, in four steps.

No jargon, no surprises. You always know where we are, and the flat quote doesn’t move along the way.

1. We map it out

You tell me what you sell, how many products you have, and how you want to take payments and ship. I come back with one flat quote and a clear timeline — before anything is built.

2. I design & build it

I build the store on Shopify around your brand, load your products, set up payments, shipping and tax, and make it fast on a phone. You review real progress, not empty mockups.

3. We test every order

We run real end-to-end test orders — cart, checkout, tax, shipping and the confirmation email — so on launch day the first real order lands perfectly.

4. We launch & I show you

We connect your domain, go live, and I walk you through adding products, fulfilling orders and reading your sales. The store is in your name, with optional ongoing care if you want it.
