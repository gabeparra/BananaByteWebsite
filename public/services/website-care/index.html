What’s covered

All the boring-but-important stuff — handled for you.

Every plan includes the items below. No fine print, no surprise “add-ons” — if it’s part of caring for your site, it’s part of the plan.

Software & plugin updates

Your site’s core, themes, and plugins stay current — tested before they go live, so an update never breaks your real page.

Security & backups

Automatic backups on a regular schedule plus active security hardening. If anything goes sideways, I restore a clean version fast — no lost content.

Uptime monitoring

Your site is checked around the clock. If it ever goes down, I hear about it first — usually before you do — and I’m already on the fix while you’re still none the wiser.

Monthly content edits

New hours, a price change, a swapped photo, a reworded paragraph? You text me and I make it happen. Every plan includes a set amount of small edits each month — no tickets, no waiting.

A set amount included each month

Priority support

As a plan client you go to the front of the line. Email or text me and you get a reply from a real human — not a bot, not “please allow 5–7 business days.”

A report you actually understand

Once a month you get a short, plain-English summary: what got updated, what got backed up, and how your site is doing. No confusing dashboards, no jargon.

How it works

Simple by design. One monthly plan, cancel anytime.

No long lock-ins, no penalties for leaving. You pay month to month and stay because it works, not because you’re trapped.

1

We pick a plan

We look at your site together and pick a care level that fits its size and how busy it is. No packages full of stuff you don’t need.

2

I handle it, quietly

Each month I run the updates, backups, and checks in the background, and make your edits whenever you send them. You do nothing.

3

Cancel anytime

Needs change? Move up, move down, or cancel with a single message. Your site is always yours — it’s never held hostage.

This is a recurring monthly plan — not the build itself.

Website care is a separate monthly fee from the one-time project to build or redesign your site. What does it cost? It depends on the size of the site, whether it’s a store, and how busy it is — so I give you one clear, flat number before anything starts. See pricing or ask me for a quote.
