What you get

A careful rebuild — not a wipe-and-pray.

A refresh should fix what’s broken without throwing away what already works for you. Here’s what comes with every redesign:

A quick, honest audit

I start by looking at your current site with fresh eyes: what confuses people, what loads slow, what breaks on phones. You get a plain-language summary — no jargon to nod along to.

Modern, responsive redesign

I rebuild the site with a clean, current design that looks just as sharp on a phone as on a big screen. I design around how customers actually find you — not a generic template.

Speed & mobile fixes

Heavy images, bloated code, and broken forms make people bounce. I tighten load times, fix the layout on small screens, and get every button and form working the way it should.

Content carried over

Your copy, photos, reviews, and Google ranking come across to the new site — cleaned up and reorganized, not abandoned. You don’t start from zero or lose what already ranks.

Keep what’s working

If a page already brings you customers, or there’s a section you love, I don’t touch it just to touch it. I keep what performs and rebuild what gets in the way — you decide what goes in each bucket.

Clearer calls-to-action

A pretty site that doesn’t tell people what to do is just decoration. I add clear buttons — call, book, buy, message — right where people are ready to act, so more visitors turn into real leads.

Signs your site needs a refresh.

If one or more of these hit a little too close to home, your site is probably costing you work instead of bringing it in.

It looks dated

Your site looks ten years old, and that tells people your business might be too. A dated design plants doubt before you’ve said a word.

It’s not mobile-friendly

More than half your visitors arrive on a phone. If they have to pinch, zoom, and drag just to read you, most of them simply leave.

It loads slow

Every extra second of load time sends visitors to a competitor. If your page is slow to show up, you’re losing people before they see anything.

It’s hard to update

Changing a price, an hour, or a photo has become such a hassle that you’ve stopped bothering. Your site should be easy to keep current, not a headache.

It’s not bringing leads

You have a site, but the phone isn’t ringing and the inbox is quiet. If it isn’t generating calls, bookings, or messages, it isn’t doing its job.

It makes you wince

You avoid sending people your own link because it doesn’t represent how good you actually are. That feeling alone is reason enough to fix it.

How it works

Four clear steps, no surprises.

You know exactly what’s happening and when, start to finish. No vanishing for weeks and coming back with something you didn’t ask for.

Audit

I go through your current site and tell you, in plain language, what works, what’s broken, and what’s costing you customers — with no obligation to continue.

Step 02

Plan

We agree on what stays, what gets rebuilt, and how the result should look and feel. You get the full scope and one flat quote before any work begins.

Step 03

Redesign

Step 04

Launch

We launch the new site carefully so you don’t lose traffic or ranking, and I hand you the keys. It’s yours, code and accounts included.
