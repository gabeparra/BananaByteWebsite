What it does

Four ways your business answers — even when you can’t.

It isn’t one thing — it’s a small system working together: it answers, captures the details, and tells you. I build it around your business and switch on only the pieces that actually earn their keep.

Bilingual chat assistant on your site

A chat window on your own site that answers the usual questions — hours, pricing, location, services — and books appointments or captures the lead, in English and Spanish, 24/7. When it doesn’t know something, it grabs the contact info so you can close.

Missed-call text-back

When you’re busy and can’t pick up, the caller instantly gets a text from you: “Sorry I missed you — I’m with a customer, how can I help?” Most people stick around instead of dialing the next name on the list.

Automatic review requests

After a finished job or a closed sale, the system sends the customer a friendly message asking for a Google review — at the right moment, while they’re still happy. More stars, without you having to remember.

Lead alerts to your phone or inbox

Every time the assistant captures a new lead, it lands on you right away — by text or email — with the name, what they want, and how to reach them. Nothing gets stranded in a dashboard you never open.

The customer you don’t answer today calls someone else tomorrow.

Most small businesses lose customers in the gaps: the call that came in while your hands were full, the 10 PM message, the person who wrote in Spanish and never heard back. This system catches exactly those moments — and serves your English-speaking AND Spanish-speaking customers the same, automatically.
