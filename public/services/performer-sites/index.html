On the bill

Everything your act needs, in one place.

The pieces a working performer actually uses — built by hand, no bloated builders, no mystery monthly fees. Take what you need and leave the rest.

01

Marquee-style design

A front page that feels like seeing your name up in lights — your photo, your voice and your energy up top, ready to land in the first second on a phone or a big screen.

02

Show & tour calendar

Upcoming dates with city, venue, time and a ticket button that always points to the right show. You update it in seconds and past dates drop off on their own — no dead links the night of a gig.

03

Booking & inquiry form Key

A booking form that lands straight in your inbox with date, location and budget, so you can reply in minutes and never lose a gig to a slow response. No dashboards, no middlemen — just you and whoever wants to book you.

04

Clip reels & video

Your best sets, rounds or mixes front and center — embedded so they load fast and look sharp. Bookers see in ten seconds exactly why they should reserve you.

05

Press kit / EPK

A clean one-sheet bookers can view or download: bio, hi-res photos, credits, tech needs and links. Everything a venue emails you for, ready on a single page.

06

Mailing list & social

A mailing-list signup that grows after every show, plus all your social links in one spot — so you own your audience and pack the room next time, without leaning on anyone’s algorithm.

The process

From first chat to opening night, in four steps.

Simple, jargon-free and light on your time. You perform; I handle the site.

01

We talk about the act

A quick call or a few messages: what you do, where you want to get booked, and what you already have — clips, photos, dates. No pressure.

02

You get a flat quote

I send one number and a clear timeline before I touch the keyboard. You approve it and we start — no hourly meter, no surprises at the end.

03

I build it

I build your site — marquee, calendar, reel, EPK and booking form — and show it to you live. You review, I refine until it feels like you.

04

It opens — and it’s yours

We launch it on your domain, I hand over the keys and show you how to update dates and clips yourself. Code, domain and accounts — all yours from day one.
