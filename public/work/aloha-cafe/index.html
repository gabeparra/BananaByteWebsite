Our Story

A little slice of the islands, two blocks off Colonial.

Aloha Café started in 2019 when Malia & Theo Kahale traded the trade winds of Hilo for a sleepy corner of Orlando's Mills 50 district. They missed the slow island mornings — the kind where the coffee is from down the road, the malasadas are still warm, and nobody's in a hurry.

So they built one. Every cup is poured by hand, the cold brew steeps for sixteen hours, and the banana bread recipe is straight from Malia's tūtū. Pull up a rattan chair, kick off your slippahs, and stay a while. You're 'ohana here.
