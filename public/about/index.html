[ About ]

Meet the founder.

One local person who answers the phone, gives you a straight answer, and shows up when promised. That’s the whole company — and that’s the point.

Gabe · Founder

Hi, I’m Gabe.

I run BANANAbyte myself — right here in Orlando, Florida. When you reach out, you’re talking to the same person who will diagnose the problem, give you the price, and do the work. There’s no call center, no middleman, and nobody handing your job off to a stranger.

[ Why I started this ]

Tech help shouldn’t feel like a fight.

I started BANANAbyte because I kept hearing the same three complaints about “the computer guy” — and I knew it could be done a better way.

Talked down to

People walk out of tech shops feeling dumb instead of helped. I explain what’s wrong in plain words, and I never make you feel bad for asking.

Overcharged

Surprise fees and padded invoices are everywhere. You get the price before I start, and the number you approve is the number you pay.

Ghosted

A “we’ll call you back” that never comes is the worst. I answer my messages, give you a real timeline, and tell you if I’m running late.

[ My promise ]

Three things I’ll always do.

No fine print needed — just the way I work with every single customer, the same way I’d want to be treated.

Honest advice

If a repair costs more than the device is worth, I’ll tell you. Sometimes the right answer is “don’t spend the money,” and you’ll hear that from me too.

Plain English

No buzzwords, no acronyms thrown at you to sound smart. Just clear answers about what’s wrong, what I’ll do, and what it means for you.

A fair price you approve up front

The quote is free and the price is locked in before any work begins. No surprise add-ons, no “while I was in there” charges — you decide before I touch a thing.

[ Who I help ]

Small businesses and families alike.

Whether you’re running a shop or just trying to get your photos off an old laptop, you get the same patience and the same fair treatment.

Small businesses

Cafés, shops, and offices that need their computers, networks, and websites to just work — without an IT department. I’m the on-call tech who keeps you running.

Families & seniors

Patient, friendly help with the computer, phone, or tablet — setting things up, fixing what’s broken, and showing you how, at your pace. I happily help seniors and anyone who’d rather just have it handled.
