[ Good to know ]

Pricing questions, answered.

Do you charge for quotes?

No — quotes are always free and there’s no obligation. Tell us what’s going on by text, WhatsApp, or email, and we’ll give you an honest estimate before any work starts. You only pay if you approve the price and we do the job.

What’s your service area? Do you travel?

We’re based in Orlando, FL and serve the greater Orlando area — including Winter Park, Kissimmee, and nearby neighborhoods — with on-site visits. Many issues we can also solve remotely from anywhere. If you’re a bit farther out, just ask: a small travel fee may apply, and we’ll always tell you up front before you commit.

How do I pay?

Whatever’s easiest for you. We accept cash, major cards, and common apps like Zelle, Venmo, and Cash App. For most jobs payment is due when the work is finished and you’re happy; larger projects like a website may be split into a deposit and a final payment, spelled out clearly before we begin.

Do you guarantee the work?

Yes. If something we fixed acts up again shortly after, we’ll come back and make it right — that’s on us. Some things, like failing hardware or recovering severely damaged drives, we can’t promise miracles on, and we’ll always be honest with you about that before you spend a dollar. Our goal is simple: you should feel the work was worth it.

Ready for a real number?

Tell us what’s going on and we’ll send back an honest, free quote — usually within one business day. No pressure, no obligation.
