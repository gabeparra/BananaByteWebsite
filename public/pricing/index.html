Before you ask

The questions almost everyone has.

Q. Why a flat quote?

Because an hourly meter punishes you, not me. We agree on the scope, I give you one number, and that’s the price — even if something takes me longer than I expected. You know exactly what it costs before I write a single line.

Q. What about hosting?

Your site lives on Cloudflare, which is blazing fast and usually free or pennies a month. I set it all up in your name. If you’d rather I handle upkeep, that’s the Website Care plan — separate from the build cost.

Q. Do I really own it?

Yes, really. The code, the domain, the content and every account are in your name. No platform lock-in, no holding your site hostage. If you ever want to take it somewhere else, I hand it all over — keys and all.

Q. When do I pay?

Just $300 to start — that deposit holds your spot and gets me building. From there it splits into simple milestones: about half when you approve the design direction, and the balance once the finished site is live and you’re happy. You’re never paying for work you haven’t seen, and I’m never building the whole thing on spec — most of the risk stays on my side.

Tell me about your project.

A few sentences is plenty. I’ll come back with a flat quote and honest next steps — usually within a business day.
